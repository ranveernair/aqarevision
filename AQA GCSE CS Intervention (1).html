8525Exam Intervention

Pick your weak spots. Climb to the exam question.

Questions come from real AQA past papers (8525, 8520, older AQA and OCR, mapped to the current specification), the 8525 topic workbooks, and extra practice questions. Before each one, a short set of leading questions walks you through the thinking the mark scheme rewards. Drop marks and you get the revision video for that exact spec point.

LOLearning Outcome

By the end of this session you will be able to answer AQA GCSE Computer Science exam questions on the topics you choose, using the steps and key terms the mark scheme expects.
